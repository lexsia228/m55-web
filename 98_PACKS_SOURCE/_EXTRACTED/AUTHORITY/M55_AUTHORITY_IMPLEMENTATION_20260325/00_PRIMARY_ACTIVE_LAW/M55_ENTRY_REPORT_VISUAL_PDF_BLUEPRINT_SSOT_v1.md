## 5. Graphic and data requirements

### 5.1 Mandatory graphics
- 1 cover illustration
- 1 five-element ring
- 1 radar chart
- 1 bias gauge
- 2 to 4 icon strips / mini diagrams across later pages

### 5.2 Graphic tone
- calm
- premium but not luxury-noise
- editorial / booklet feel
- no fortune-site neon mysticism

### 5.3 What graphics must do
Graphics are not decoration only.  
They must:
- reduce text fatigue
- increase ownership feel
- make invisible structure legible
- create page volume without leaking extra deep text on free lane

## 6. Illustration system

### 6.1 Illustration style target
Preferred visual language:
- quiet symbolic editorial illustration
- low-noise geometric forms
- layered translucent shapes
- subtle metallic or paper texture feel
- restrained celestial / elemental cues
- not anime character-centric
- not generic stock watercolor

### 6.2 Illustration usage by page
- Page 1: singular identity illustration
- Page 3: essence motif
- Page 6: relation/communication motif
- optional small motifs on 4 / 5 / 7

### 6.3 Illustration must support reading, not dominate it
Image area target per page:
- cover: `40〜55%`
- inner pages with chart: `25〜40%`
- inner pages with paragraph emphasis: `15〜25%`
