For public-facing copy in free-result and product explanation contexts:
- internal canonical family key remains `dtr`
- public label must prefer `Entry Report` / `Report`

## 0. Executive judgment

無料結果は薄すぎてはならない。  
しかし、Entry Report の代用品になってもならない。

したがって、無料結果は **“理解はできるが、所有したくなる深さはまだない”** に固定する。

---

## 1. Free result mission

Free result has exactly four jobs.

1. 自分の構成が見えた、と感じさせる
2. M55 の読み方を教育する
3. Entry Report との違いを納得させる
4. 購入後の room rule を先に理解させる

---

## 2. Free result content budget

### 2.1 Total visible budget across free lane
Across Home + core free summary, visible free text should land at:
`1,100〜1,800 chars`

Hard ceiling:
`2,100 chars`

### 2.2 Budget by module
- title / 称号: `10〜30 chars`
- one-line subtitle: `35〜60 chars`
- essence summary: `280〜420 chars`
- five-element mini captions total: `180〜280 chars`
- current focus: `90〜150 chars`
- bridge to Entry Report: `80〜140 chars`
- system rule mini explainer: `160〜260 chars`

---

## 3. Mandatory free-result modules

### 3.1 Identity block
- 称号
- subtitle
- one-line core identity

### 3.2 Five-element block
- ring / radar / bias gauge
- 5 mini labels
- 1 short sentence explaining how to read the chart

### 3.3 Essence summary block
- calm, readable summary
- enough to feel personal
- must stop before deep structure becomes fully explicit

### 3.4 Current focus block
- “今この瞬間の焦点”
- short only
- not timeline-grade

### 3.5 Entry bridge block
- what gets deeper after purchase
- one static example of “無料 -> 購入後” deepening

### 3.6 System rule block
Mandatory.  
Must explain:
- 無料で見える範囲
- 購入後に広がる範囲
- 相談1回付属
- 追加相談は room 内のみ
- 最大3回まで

---

## 4. Free vs Entry boundary

### 4.1 Free may show
- structure name
- chart silhouette
- brief essence summary
- focus summary
- chapter headings of deeper report
- one teaser sentence per hidden chapter

### 4.2 Free must not show
- full chapter bodies
- long-form narrative paragraphs
- complete relationship/work/growth section bodies
- timeline comparison body
- consult-room answer quality simulation in full

### 4.3 Required differentiation sentence
A user must be able to understand in one glance:

Free = 見取り図  
Entry Report = 同じ本質を構造化して所有する版

---

## 5. System rule education in free lane

### 5.1 Why mandatory
M55 is not “generic AI chat”.  
Users must understand product law before paying.

### 5.2 Required system explanation card
Card title candidate:
- M55の読み方
- 使い方の流れ
- 仕組み

Required bullets in user language:
- 無料では基礎の見取り図が見えます
- 購入後は同じ本質が深く整理されます
- レポートには相談1回がつきます
- 追加相談はそのレポートの部屋だけで行えます
- 1レポートの相談は最大3回です

### 5.3 Tone rule
- quiet
- explicit
- non-threatening
- non-scarcity

---

## 6. Anti-cannibalization rules

### 6.1 Never do these
- publish the deepest insight paragraph in free
- reveal all chapter titles and all summary sentences together
- simulate a full DTR page as if already owned
- expose the complete PDF layout with readable copy
- let free result exceed entry report in total semantic density

### 6.2 Required asymmetry
Free should excel in:
- instant readability
- visual explanation
- curiosity

Entry should dominate in:
- ownership
- chapter depth
- narrative continuity
- structured body
- consult privilege

---

## 7. Free-result visual language

### 7.1 What to show clearly
- charts
- headings
- short labels
- first paragraph
- chapter skeleton

### 7.2 What to soften / blur
- second and third paragraphs of deeper themes
- full page spread previews
- advanced section bodies
- relationship/work/growth long blocks

### 7.3 Blur wording
Do not say “missing” or “locked because you are not premium enough.”
Say instead:
- 購入後に深く読める項目
- この先はレポートで整理されます
- 同じ本質をさらに詳しく読む

---

## 8. Relationship to future compatibility and timeline families

Free lane must not imply that current hero is compatibility or timeline DTR.

Allowed future mentions:
- 関係の読みは今後追加予定
- 時間軸の読みは保存されたデータが揃ったときに開く

Forbidden:
- compatibility current hero positioning
- timeline DTR current public CTA
- Standard rail as co-hero

無料結果は、見える・分かる・欲しくなる、の3つを満たす。
