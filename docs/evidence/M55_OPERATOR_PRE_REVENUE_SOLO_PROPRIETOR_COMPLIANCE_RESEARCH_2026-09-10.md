## Primary-source findings

### NTA — opening / blue return
- individual-business opening notification: due by the income-tax return deadline for the year containing the business-start event under current NTA guidance;
- blue-return application: generally March 15, or within two months of starting a business on/after January 16.

Sources:
- https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/tetsuzuki/shinsei/annai/shinkoku/annai/42.htm
- https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/tetsuzuki/shinsei/annai/shinkoku/annai/09.htm

### NTA — books / business-income classification
- business-income activities require books/document preservation even where no tax return is otherwise needed;
- business vs business-related miscellaneous income is judged by social convention/facts;
- absence of books strongly weighs against business-income treatment;
- persistent small revenue / persistent losses with no profit-improvement activity can require individual judgment.

Sources:
- https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/shinkoku/kojin_jigyo/index.htm
- https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kihon/shotoku/04/09.htm
- https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kihon/shotoku/kaisei/221007/pdf/02.pdf

### NTA — blue loss
Blue-return business losses can generally be carried forward for three years when requirements and filings are met.

Source:
- https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/koho/kurashi/html/01_2.htm

### NTA — opening/development costs
NTA training materials recognize opening costs and certain development costs as deferred assets with specific amortization treatment. Individual expenses/assets require classification.

Source:
- https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/ntc/kohon/syotoku/pdf/all.pdf

### NTA — electronic transactions
Electronic invoices/receipts/contracts/orders and similar transaction data exchanged electronically must be retained electronically under the applicable electronic-bookkeeping rules.

Sources:
- https://www.nta.go.jp/law/joho-zeikaishaku/sonota/jirei/tokusetsu/pdf/0024011-003_01.pdf
- https://www.nta.go.jp/law/joho-zeikaishaku/sonota/jirei/4-3.htm

### NTA — consumption tax / invoices
- new sole proprietors generally have no base period and are ordinarily exempt in the opening year and next year, subject to exceptions;
- invoice registration is voluntary;
- registration by an otherwise exempt business creates consumption-tax filing/payment consequences;
- 2026 reform contains time-limited special treatment for certain individual businesses.

Sources:
- https://www.e-tax.nta.go.jp/toiawase/qa/e-taxweb_invoice/40.htm
- https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/zeimokubetsu/shohi/keigenzeiritsu/pdf/sinkijigyousha/faq.pdf
- https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/zeimokubetsu/shohi/keigenzeiritsu/invoice-review/index.htm

### NTA — source withholding
Individual payer + no salary payer generally does not withhold on remuneration/fees, except specified cases such as hostess remuneration. Blue-return family-employee salary counts as salary.

Source:
- https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/gensen/2793.htm

### JFTC — no-employee orderer
- broad ordering-business category exists regardless of employees;
- specified ordering-business category for individuals requires use of employees;
- no-employee orderer still owes Article 3 transaction-condition disclosure for a covered delegation;
- current JFTC chart shows only duty #1 for an orderer not using employees.

Sources:
- https://www.jftc.go.jp/fllaw_limited/fllaw_qa.html
- https://www.jftc.go.jp/freelancelaw_2025/

### MHLW / Japan Pension Service — zero employees
- one employee generally triggers labour-insurance/employment-insurance procedures;
- zero employees means those employer duties are not currently triggered;
- individual establishments with five+ regular employees can trigger health/pension establishment rules under current law depending on industry;
- from October 2029 the industry limitation expands/changes, with transitional rules.

Sources:
- https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/koyou_roudou/roudoukijun/hoken/index.html
- https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/kounen/tekiyo/jigyosho/20150518.html
- https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/nenkin_shikumi_009.html

### PPC — personal information
- individuals and small businesses are covered when using a personal-information database for business;
- no small-volume exemption based merely on number of people;
- security measures may be proportionate to size/risk.

Sources:
- https://www.ppc.go.jp/personalinfo/legal/guidelines_tsusoku/
- https://www.ppc.go.jp/all_faq_index/faq1-q10-5/

### CAA — Tokushoho
- paid information services can be communication sales;
- individual seller identity/address/phone rules apply;
- legal name/registered trade name is the baseline, not a mere shop name;
- request-based omission and qualifying virtual-office/platform contact methods can be used only under the official conditions.

Sources:
- https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/qa/advertising.html
- https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/what/mailorder/advertising.php

### Stripe Japan
- sole proprietors should register using personal/legal name rather than merely a trade name;
- live activation requires business profile/KYC/bank information and Tokushoho disclosure.

Source:
- https://support.stripe.com/questions/getting-started-with-stripe-a-guide-for-users-in-japan?locale=ja-JP

### Local tax
Local opening-notification requirements are jurisdiction-dependent. Tokyo is an example with a 15-day individual-business-tax start notification; do not apply that deadline nationally.

Source example:
