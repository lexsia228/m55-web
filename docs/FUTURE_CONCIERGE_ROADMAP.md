Status: INTERNAL_ROADMAP_ONLY

目的:
タロット/カード、保存データ、解析チャート、会員・サブスク、AIコンシェルジュ連続性を future roadmap として整理する。
これは current launch plan ではない。

## North-star concept
M55 should evolve from isolated one-time deliverables into a continuity system where:
- purchased content remains accessible
- AI interaction can be extended through credits or membership
- personal data and charts improve continuity
- app and web share the same entitlement backbone

## Roadmap layers
### Layer 1: Stable revenue primitives
- DTR one-time family
- AI chat credit packs
- shared purchase spine
- supportable entitlement visibility

### Layer 2: Experience-density modes
- guided card mode
- tarot/card surfaces as interaction mode, not first billing primitive
- richer interpretation surfaces
- optional premium reading add-ons

### Layer 3: Retained user state
- saved records
- trend summaries
- personal charts / analysis views
- longitudinal insights

### Layer 4: Membership / concierge
- recurring member tiers
- concierge continuity
- priority support / premium interaction
- cross-device continuity for web + app

## Constraints
- do not activate recurring publicly before separate legal/evidence/governance readiness
- do not make divinatory claims on frozen public surfaces
- do not couple concierge promise to unstable infrastructure
- preserve webhook-first truth and evidence discipline as the backbone

## Transition logic
The future app can become harder to leave only if:
- rights are durable
- balance/state is visible
- saved data compounds value
- concierge continuity is real, not copywriting only
