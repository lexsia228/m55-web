# Competitor information-architecture evidence v1

Status: RESEARCH EVIDENCE ONLY
Governing SSOT: `docs/ssot/M55_METHOD_AND_AUTHORITY_SSOT_v1.md`
Observation window: 2026-07-20 … 2026-07-29

This document records **structural observations** about how self-understanding
products in adjacent categories organise a method explanation. It exists to
answer one question: *which information does a reader look for before trusting a
method, and in what order?*

It is **not** runtime copy authority. Nothing here may be imported into product
components or method authority modules.

## Hard limits (non-negotiable)

- No competitor wording is quoted or paraphrased into product copy.
- No competitor visual assets, layouts, colour systems or typography are copied.
- No competitor theory name, framework name or trademark is used in M55 copy.
- No competitor claim is adopted. A competitor claiming validation does not make
  the claim available to M55.
- Nothing in this document raises M55's authority level.
- Do not claim undisclosed revenue, profitability, conversion rates, scientific
  validity, unpublished user counts, or causation between layout and commercial
  success.

## Entry schema

Every observed competitor entry uses:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Service name | Public product name |
| Official source URL | Official page used for the observation |
| Observation date | ISO date of the observation |
| OBSERVED FACT | What was structurally present on that page |
| M55 INFERENCE | How M55 may respond in its own authority |
| Prohibited inference / copying boundary | What must not be copied or claimed |

## Observed entries

### E1 — 16Personalities (method transparency before results framing)

- **Service name:** 16Personalities
- **Official source URL:** https://www.16personalities.com/articles/our-theory
- **Observation date:** 2026-07-28
- **OBSERVED FACT:** The official theory page places an explanation of model
  inputs and framework structure on a dedicated editorial route, separate from
  the paid upgrade surface.
- **M55 INFERENCE:** A reader expects a single canonical method page that can be
  linked from pricing and result surfaces without restating a second model.
- **Prohibited inference / copying boundary:** Do not copy theory names, type
  labels, diagrams, accuracy language, or user-count claims. Do not infer that
  M55 has psychological measurement authority.

### E2 — Gallup CliftonStrengths (input list adjacency)

- **Service name:** CliftonStrengths (Gallup)
- **Official source URL:** https://www.gallup.com/cliftonstrengths/en/253676/how-cliftonstrengths-works.aspx
- **Observation date:** 2026-07-28
- **OBSERVED FACT:** The official “how it works” page enumerates what the
  assessment uses as input before describing paid report depth.
- **M55 INFERENCE:** Listing composition inputs in daily language before the
  purchase decision reduces the sense of a hidden model.
- **Prohibited inference / copying boundary:** Do not copy Gallup trademarks,
  strength names, ranking language, scientific-validity claims, or participation
  counts.

### E3 — HumanMetrics / Jung typology style questionnaire (boundary language)

- **Service name:** HumanMetrics
- **Official source URL:** https://www.humanmetrics.com/personality/about
- **Observation date:** 2026-07-27
- **OBSERVED FACT:** The about page keeps a short non-goal / limitation statement
  near the method explanation rather than only in legal text.
- **M55 INFERENCE:** An explicit “what this does not do” section belongs on the
  canonical method route (M55 section 10) and in compact placements as the
  boundary sentence.
- **Prohibited inference / copying boundary:** Do not copy Jungian type labels,
  diagnostic wording, or accuracy percentages.

### E4 — Birth-date reading storefront pattern (depth as scope)

- **Service name:** Cafe Astrology (representative birth-date reading storefront)
- **Official source URL:** https://cafeastrology.com/astrologyreports.html
- **Observation date:** 2026-07-27
- **OBSERVED FACT:** Paid report tiers are described by coverage / length of the
  reading rather than by a higher “hit rate”.
- **M55 INFERENCE:** Free vs Premium should be framed as additional depth and
  readable range, never as improved accuracy.
- **Prohibited inference / copying boundary:** Do not copy astrology house/sign
  theory names, predictive claims, or fortune-telling accuracy language into M55.

### E5 — StrengthsFinder-adjacent coaching report pattern (privacy adjacency)

- **Service name:** VIA Character Strengths
- **Official source URL:** https://www.viacharacter.org/account/register
- **Observation date:** 2026-07-26
- **OBSERVED FACT:** Account / save messaging sits next to the assessment entry,
  so storage expectations appear before the reader invests answers.
- **M55 INFERENCE:** Method section 9 (save and privacy) should stay on the
  canonical method page, not only in `/legal/privacy`.
- **Prohibited inference / copying boundary:** Do not copy VIA trademarks,
  virtue labels, research-participant counts, or clinical-validity claims.

## Structural findings (derived from the entries above)

### F1 — Method position

OBSERVED FACT: Trustworthy-feeling products place method explanation before
price comparison.

M55 INFERENCE: HOME four-step model sits between general value explanation and
Premium comparison; `/dtr/lp` shows free/Premium difference before plan cards.

### F2 — Input transparency

OBSERVED FACT: Explicit input lists reduce “hidden model” suspicion.

M55 INFERENCE: `/how-m55-works` section 2 lists the nine composition inputs in
daily Japanese.

### F3 — Separation of stable and current

OBSERVED FACT: Products that blur fixed traits with current-state answers invite
horoscope readings.

M55 INFERENCE: Stable foundation vs current answers is step 1 of the model and
section 1 of the method page.

### F4 — Reproducibility as consistency, not accuracy

OBSERVED FACT: Same-input/same-output language reads as fairness when not dressed
as accuracy.

M55 INFERENCE: Canonical reproducibility sentence describes processing
consistency only.

### F5 — Explicit non-goals

OBSERVED FACT: Credible products keep a short non-goal section near the method.

M55 INFERENCE: Section 10 + compact boundary sentence.

### F6 — Depth as scope

OBSERVED FACT: “More ground” reads honest; “more accurate” reads like upselling.

M55 INFERENCE: Free/Premium difference copy denies hit-rate improvement.

### F7 — One canonical method page

OBSERVED FACT: Multiple overlapping how-it-works pages drift.

M55 INFERENCE: `/how-m55-works` is the only detailed method route.

### F8 — Privacy adjacency

OBSERVED FACT: Birth-date products benefit when save/share rules sit with method.

M55 INFERENCE: Section 9 on the canonical method page.

## Non-adoptions

Observed and deliberately **not** adopted:

- validation/accuracy badges and percentage figures
- named psychological frameworks as authority
- user-count or participant-count social proof
- expert-supervision claims without documented supervision
- future-prediction or other-person-feeling framing
- competitor wording, visual assets, theory names or trademarks

Each of these remains LEVEL 2 or LEVEL 3 under the method SSOT and is unavailable
to M55 until M55's own evidence exists.
