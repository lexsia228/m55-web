# M55_CHART_LIBRARY_SSOT_v1

AUTHORITY: SECONDARY SUPPORTING LAW
Status: READY-FOR-CURSOR
Priority: HIGH
Scope: chart types / chart usage by lane / free vs paid boundary / wording discipline
Intent: M55 の graph / chart / data modules を free lane と Entry Report で安全に使い分ける

---

## 0. Executive judgment

M55 は chart を score として使わない。
Chart は「比較優位」ではなく、**構成と偏りを理解させる可視化辞書**である。

したがって chart library は、
- free lane で見せてよい chart
- Entry Report で深く使える chart
- future timeline family でしか使わない chart
を分けて固定する。

---

## 1. Core chart families

### 1.1 Five-element ring / donut
Role:
- instant composition understanding
- easiest first-read chart

### 1.2 Five-element radar
Role:
- shape-memory
- structure silhouette

### 1.3 Bias gauge
Role:
- quiet excess / deficit expression
- explain “stronger / quieter / balanced” without ranking

### 1.4 Title card matrix
Role:
- title / trait family preview
- non-numeric identity framing

### 1.5 Chapter bridge strip
Role:
- show which deeper sections exist in Entry Report
- no body leakage

### 1.6 Timeline-only strip / sequence chart
Role:
- future family only
- not current public hero lane

---

## 2. Free lane chart allowance

### 2.1 Allowed on Home / free results
- five-element ring
- five-element radar
- bias gauge
- title card
- tiny preview strip of future chapters with labels only

### 2.2 Forbidden on Home / free results
- multi-page comparative atlas
- dense interpretive chart notes
- timeline sequence chart
- stored-history comparison chart
- consult transcript visualization
- any chart that effectively reveals full Entry Report structure

### 2.3 Free lane chart quantity rule
Recommended visible chart count in free lane:
`2〜4`

Hard ceiling:
`5`

Reason:
If too many charts appear, free lane begins to feel like the paid booklet.

---

## 3. Entry Report chart allowance

### 3.1 Allowed in Entry Report
- five-element ring with annotation
- five-element radar with narrative caption
- bias gauge with interpretation note
- title card block
- section opener graphic
- chapter bridge graphic
- ownership page mini chart

### 3.2 Target density
Entry Report may feel like a mini booklet.
Charts should increase page richness, not overwhelm reading.

Recommended major chart count:
`4〜6`

### 3.3 Hard rule
Chart count may increase page density,
but chart logic may not replace body reasoning.

---

## 4. Future timeline family charts

This chapter is not current public truth.

### 4.1 Reserve for future family only
- comparative period chart
- past-present-future bridge chart
- stored-history accumulation graph
- repeat-pattern sequence view
- period change strip

### 4.2 Why reserved
These charts imply time-axis leverage.
They must not appear in static Entry hero lane.

---

## 5. Wording law

### 5.1 Allowed wording
- 構成
- 比重
- かたより
- 強め
- 静かめ
- バランス
- いま見えている層

### 5.2 Forbidden wording
- score
- rank
- grade
- above average
- superior
- elite
- percentage-of-people framing

### 5.3 Numeric caution
Percentages may exist inside chart mechanics,
but user-facing emphasis must remain on composition, not performance.

---

## 6. Anti-cannibalization law

Charts in free lane must create:
- understanding
- curiosity
- confidence that the system saw something real

Charts in free lane must not create:
- full ownership feeling
- “I already got the report” feeling
- substitute-depth for paid report

---

## 7. Final command

Use charts as a quiet evidence layer.
Do not use charts as a score game.
Do not let free charts cross into paid-body substitution.
