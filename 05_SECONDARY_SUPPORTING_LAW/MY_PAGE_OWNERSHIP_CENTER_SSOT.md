# M55 Web My Page Ownership Center SSOT

Status: DRAFT FOR FREEZE
Mode: ownership / continuity / account law
Scope: release-night MVP, wave A, future growth

---

## 1. Objective

My Page must become the stable ownership center of M55 Web.
It must reassure users that what they unlocked, purchased, saved, or may need support for has a clear home.

---

## 2. Non-negotiable constraints

- My Page is not a promotional wall.
- My Page is not a broad marketplace.
- My Page must keep trust, continuity, and clarity above monetization pressure.
- Purchased items, account help, support, and contract visibility must be easy to find.

---

## 3. Mandatory My Page blocks

The following block order is frozen.

1. **Account / identity block**
   - nickname or user identity confirmation
   - short privacy reassurance if needed

2. **Owned content block**
   - purchased reports
   - owned DTR cards
   - reopen / open CTA where applicable

3. **Status / continuity block**
   - reflection pending if relevant
   - remaining follow-up usage if relevant
   - expiry / availability if relevant

4. **Support / help block**
   - missing access
   - statement recognition
   - deletion / retention
   - support CTA

5. **Contract / policy block**
   - terms
   - refund
   - tokushoho
   - subscription or account management where relevant

---

## 4. What My Page must communicate

The user must understand:
- what they currently own
- what is still reflecting
- what actions they can take now
- where to get help
- that they are not lost after purchase

---

## 5. Future-proofing law

Future additions may appear inside My Page only if they support ownership, continuity, history, or account trust.

Allowed:
- purchased content shelves
- follow-up usage counts
- archive cards
- contract/account center

Disallowed:
- noisy upsell walls
- unrelated feature dumping
- homepage replacement inside My Page

---

## 6. Visual law

My Page must use the same quiet premium card family as support, success, and root trust surfaces.
It must feel reliable, not experimental.

---

## 7. Implementation targets

Primary files likely affected:
- `app/my/page.tsx`
- purchased report card components
- support/account link blocks

---

## 8. Freeze summary

My Page is where M55 proves ownership is real.
If a user can always recover their place from My Page, the ownership center is correct.
