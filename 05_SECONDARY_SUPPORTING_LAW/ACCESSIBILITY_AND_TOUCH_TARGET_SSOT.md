# M55 Web Accessibility and Touch Target SSOT

Status: DRAFT FOR FREEZE
Mode: usability constitution
Scope: release-night MVP and beyond

---

## 1. Objective

M55 Web must be comfortable across phone, tablet, and desktop, with globally understandable interaction behavior.

---

## 2. Minimum interaction law

- minimum touch target: `44px x 44px`
- nav item target: `>= 56px` height
- CTA target: `>= 48px` height
- close buttons / menu buttons: `>= 44px`

---

## 3. Contrast law

Primary text and key interactive elements must maintain readable contrast.
Accent color must not reduce legibility.

---

## 4. State visibility law

Hover-only information is forbidden for core flows.
All important states must remain visible on touch devices.

---

## 5. Keyboard law

Core routes and actions must remain reachable without pointer-only assumptions.

---

## 6. Loading / empty / error law

These states must be readable, centered in the content frame, and must not trap the user.

---

## 7. Motion law

Motion must be restrained.
No unnecessary large parallax or distracting transitions in release-night MVP.

---

## 8. Freeze summary

If it is not easy to tap, scan, and recover from errors, it is not premium.
